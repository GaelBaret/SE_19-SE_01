About

My name is Gael Baret. Welcome to my blog — a place where I share my thoughts, experiences, ideas, and the things I’m learning along the way.

I created this blog to document my journey, explore topics that genuinely interest me, and share useful insights that might help or inspire others. You’ll find a mix of personal experiences, practical advice, opinions and discoveries as I continue learning and building new things.

My goal is simple:

  • Keep things useful
  • Keep things genuine
  • Keep things easy to understand

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you find something here worth reading.
